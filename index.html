FAQ

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

How long does a website take?+

Timing depends on the scope, content and revisions. A focused website can move quickly; larger custom builds take longer.

What information do you need from me?+

Your business details, goals, services, preferred content, contact details, brand assets and any examples of styles you like.

Do you provide revisions?+

Yes. One revision is included in the listed plans. Major additional changes may cost extra.

How does payment work?+

50% advance to begin and 50% on final delivery.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes. Existing websites can be reviewed and redesigned with a new structure, visual direction and responsive experience.

Do you build mobile-friendly websites?+

Yes. Responsive behavior is built into the website structure for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Can I request custom features?+

Yes. Custom functionality can be discussed before the project begins and may require a custom quote.
